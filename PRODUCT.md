# Product

## Register

product

## Users

One person tracking their own recurring costs on their phone. Context: quick
glance to answer "what's about to charge me, and what do I still owe?" — opened
briefly, often from a home-screen PWA icon, sometimes offline. Not a team tool,
not a finance power-user tool. The job: never be surprised by a renewal or a
missed installment payment.

## Product Purpose

A phone-first PWA that tracks subscription renewals and installment plans and
sends server-scheduled email reminders even when the app is closed. Success =
the user trusts it enough to stop mentally tracking renewals. Everything runs on
free tiers ($0); that constraint is a hard product boundary, not a nice-to-have.

## Brand Personality

Calm, trustworthy, out-of-the-way. Three words: quiet, dependable, quick. It
should feel like a well-made utility that disappears into the task — closer to a
native settings screen than a fintech dashboard. No hype, no gamification beyond
a small "paid off" moment of relief.

## Anti-references

- Fintech dashboards that shout with charts, gradients, and big hero metrics.
- Budgeting apps that moralize spending or push upsells.
- Generic SaaS-cream + purple-gradient AI-template look.

## Design Principles

- **Answer the one question first.** Every screen leads with what's due / owed.
- **Familiar over clever.** Standard affordances; the tool should disappear.
- **Optimistic and honest.** Actions apply instantly; failures surface plainly.
- **Small and offline-friendly.** No weight that a $0 phone utility can't carry.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Status must never be color-only (pills also carry text/icon).
Touch targets sized for one-handed phone use. Respect reduced-motion. Legible
muted text — no light-gray-on-tint body copy.
